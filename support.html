FAQ

Typing my own line doesn't work.
Typed lines are read by Apple Intelligence, which must be supported by your device and turned on in Settings → Apple Intelligence. On other devices you can play with the suggested lines.

How do I change the language?
Tap the English / 한국어 button at the top right.

What about my data?
See the privacy policy. IKKEUL collects no personal data.
